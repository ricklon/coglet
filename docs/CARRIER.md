# Carrier board

> **Design, not yet built.** See [the step-by-step guide](CARRIER-STEPS.md) for the order of work. Generated by [`tools/carrier/carrier.py`](../tools/carrier/carrier.py).

The XIAO, the MAX98357A amp and the INMP441 mic go on an **ElectroCookie mini solderable breadboard** (1/4 size, 38.1 × 50.8 mm), the board the N20 drive in cam-rotation-starter uses. The PCA9685 and the speaker stay off it, on short leads; the servo supply goes straight to the PCA9685's terminal block.

## The board

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 38.1 × 50.8 mm (1.5 × 2.0 in), 1.6 mm FR4, plated through-holes |
| Holes | 0.1 in (2.54 mm) grid, rows 1–17 |
| Strips | Each row is two strips of five joined holes, **A–E** and **F–J**, either side of a 0.3 in centre channel. No power rails. |
| Underside | The print is **mirrored**: A↔J, B↔I, C↔H, D↔G, E↔F, same row. To check your own board, push a wire into top A2, turn the board over and read the hole it comes out of. |

## Placement

The XIAO's pin rows are 15.24 mm apart (0.6 in), the same as columns D and H. Check this against your module before soldering.

| Part | Where | Pins |
|---|---|---|
| XIAO ESP32 (S3 Sense, C3 or C6), component side up, USB-C toward row 1 | Rows 4–12 over columns D–H | D0–D5 at D5–D10, 5V H5, GND H6, 3V3 H7, D10 H8. **D6, D7, D8 and D9 stay out.** |
| MAX98357A amp, standing on its header | Column A, rows 1–7 | Vin A1, GND A2, DIN A5, BCLK A6, LRC A7. SD and GAIN have no pin. *Pin order assumed.* |
| INMP441 mic, standing on its header | Column J, rows 12–17 | SCK J12, WS J13, SD J14, L/R J15, VDD J16, GND J17. *Pin order assumed.* Where its sound port faces is open. |
| Lead to the PCA9685, a 5-pin header | Column J, rows 6–10 | GND J6, VCC J7, /OE J8, SDA J9, SCL J10 |

The amp's DIN, BCLK and LRC sit in the same strips as the XIAO's D0, D1 and D2, so they need no links; the GND, 3V3 and D10 pins of the lead sit in the XIAO's right-hand strips for the same reason.

## Links (underside)

Ten links of 30 AWG stranded wire, ends tinned, run on the underside. 6 pairs cross; that is fine with insulated wire, but keep every link flat.

| Link | Colour | From | To | Job | Cut (estimated) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L3 | black | F15 (printed E15) | F17 (printed E17) | Ties the mic's L/R pin to its GND pin, so it drives the left slot. | 20 mm |
| L1 | violet | E9 (printed F9) | F9 (printed E9) | SDA: D4's strip to the lead's SDA pin. | 20 mm |
| L2 | grey | E10 (printed F10) | F10 (printed E10) | SCL: D5's strip to the lead's SCL pin. | 20 mm |
| L4 | red | E1 (printed F1) | F5 (printed E5) | +5 V from the XIAO's 5V strip to the amp's Vin. | 30 mm |
| L5 | black | E2 (printed F2) | F6 (printed E6) | Ground from the XIAO's GND strip to the amp's GND. | 30 mm |
| L9 | green | E7 (printed F7) | F13 (printed E13) | I2S word select: D2's strip (the amp's LRC) to the mic's WS. | 35 mm |
| L10 | blue | E8 (printed F8) | F14 (printed E14) | Mic data: D3's strip to the mic's SD. | 35 mm |
| L8 | yellow | E6 (printed F6) | F12 (printed E12) | I2S bit clock: D1's strip (the amp's BCLK) to the mic's SCK. | 35 mm |
| L6 | orange | F16 (printed E16) | G7 (printed D7) | 3.3 V from the XIAO's 3V3 strip to the mic's VDD. | 40 mm |
| L7 | black | G17 (printed D17) | G6 (printed D6) | Ground from the XIAO's GND strip to the mic's GND. | 45 mm |

Cut lengths are the routed length × 1.15 + 10 mm, rounded up to 5 mm. There is no 3D model, so they are estimates: trim each link to fit.

## Where it goes

**Pending.** The mechanical design decides where the carrier mounts, where the mic faces and how much height the standing amp and mic may take. The S3 Sense's camera board stacks on top of the XIAO and needs clearance above it.

## Assumptions to confirm on the bench

1. The amp breakout's pin order (assumed Vin, GND, SD, GAIN, DIN, BCLK, LRC).
2. The mic breakout's pin order (assumed SCK, WS, SD, L/R, VDD, GND).
3. That your perfboard's underside print is mirrored as described.
4. That the XIAO's rows are 0.6 in apart on your module.
5. The cut lengths.
