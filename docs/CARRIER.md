# Carrier board

> **Design, matched to a prototype in progress.** The XIAO's and amp's positions and the mic's and PCA9685's signal wires follow a board being built; the underside links and the power wire holes are the design's. See [the step-by-step guide](CARRIER-STEPS.md) for the order of work. Generated by [`tools/carrier/carrier.py`](../tools/carrier/carrier.py).

The XIAO and the MAX98357A amp go on an **ElectroCookie mini solderable breadboard** (1/4 size, 38.1 × 50.8 mm), the board the N20 drive in cam-rotation-starter uses. The INMP441 mic (75 mm wires) and the PCA9685 (90 mm wires) sit off it, where the robot needs them, and their wires land straight in the carrier's holes. The speaker wires to the amp's terminal; the servo supply goes straight to the PCA9685's terminal block.

The amp lies flat with its pins in column D rows 11 to 17 and its body toward column J. Jumpers I2 to H8 and I3 to H9, and E8 to F8 and E9 to F9, make row 8 a ground rail and row 9 a 3.3 V rail. The mic's signal wires land in C2, C3 and C4 and the PCA9685's in C5, B6 and J4; both parts' power wires share B8 (ground) and B9 (3.3 V). ## The board

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 38.1 × 50.8 mm (1.5 × 2.0 in), 1.6 mm FR4, plated through-holes |
| Holes | 0.1 in (2.54 mm) grid, rows 1–17 |
| Strips | Each row is two strips of five joined holes, **A–E** and **F–J**, either side of a 0.3 in centre channel. No power rails. |
| Underside | The print is **mirrored**: A↔J, B↔I, C↔H, D↔G, E↔F, same row. To check your own board, push a wire into top A2, turn the board over and read the hole it comes out of. |
| Mounting | Four Ø2.2 mm (M2) corner holes, 31.8 × 44.5 mm apart (as measured on this board in cam-rotation-starter). Their screws and supports cover A1, J1, A17 and J17, so those holes stay empty and no link runs within 3.5 mm of a corner hole. The two M3 holes in the channel aren't used: the row-1 one is under the XIAO and the row-17 one under the amp. |

## Placement

Seeed's land pattern puts the XIAO's pin rows 15.25 mm apart (0.6 in), the same as columns D and H, with its body 17.8 × 21 mm centred on the pins. Its pins sit in rows 1–7, so its USB-C end is at the board's row-1 edge: pin 1 is 2.9 mm from the body's end, which leaves the body about 2.2 mm inside the board edge. USB is the robot's 5 V input and its console, so the cable stays plugged in and has to come straight in from that edge.

| Part | Where | Pins |
|---|---|---|
| XIAO ESP32 (S3 Sense, C3 or C6), component side up, USB-C at the row-1 edge | Rows 1–7 over columns D–H | D0–D5 at D1–D6, 5V H1, GND H2, 3V3 H3, D10 H4. **D6, D7, D8 and D9 stay out.** |
| MAX98357A amp, flat on a straight header, body toward column J | Column D, rows 11–17 | Vin D17, GND D16, SD D15, GAIN D14, DIN D13, BCLK D12, LRC D11. SD connects to nothing; GAIN is tied to Vin (6 dB). |
| INMP441 mic, off the board on 75 mm wires, L/R tied to GND on the mic | Wire ends | SCK C2, WS C3, SD C4, GND B8, VDD B9 |
| PCA9685, off the board on 90 mm wires | Wire ends | SDA C5, SCL B6, GND B8, VCC B9, /OE J4 |

Every mic and PCA9685 wire lands in a strip that already carries its net, so none of them needs a link: the signals in the XIAO's own strips, the power wires on two rails that four component-side jumpers make out of rows 8 and 9.

| Jumper | From | To | Colour | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | I2 | H8 | colour not recorded | Ground from the XIAO's GND strip (row 2) to row 8. |
| T2 | I3 | H9 | colour not recorded | 3.3 V from the XIAO's 3V3 strip (row 3) to row 9. |
| T3 | E8 | F8 | orange | Joins row 8 across the channel: a ground rail on both halves. |
| T4 | E9 | F9 | orange | Joins row 9 across the channel: a 3.3 V rail on both halves. |

The amp's pins sit in rows 11–17, away from the XIAO's, so L1–L5 carry its three I2S lines, 5 V and ground through the centre channel, and L6 ties GAIN to Vin. Holes under the amp's body (roughly columns E–I, rows 10–17) can't take a wire from the top.

| mic SCK | C2 | 75 mm | white |
| mic WS | C3 | 75 mm | blue |
| mic SD | C4 | 75 mm | yellow |
| mic GND | B8 | 75 mm | black |
| mic VDD | B9 | 75 mm | red |
| PCA9685 SDA | C5 | 90 mm | green |
| PCA9685 SCL | B6 | 90 mm | yellow |

## Links (underside)

6 links of 30 AWG stranded wire, ends tinned, run on the underside, laid in the order below. 1 pairs cross; that is fine with insulated wire, but keep every link flat.

The order matters: each link keeps at least 1.6 mm (centre to centre) from every hole soldered after it, the module pins included, so the iron never meets a wire's insulation. Links that don't cross keep 0.8 mm apart. `--check` enforces both. Link ends under the XIAO or the amp are trimmed flush on the component side.

| Link | Colour | From | To | Job | Cut (estimated) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | green | E3 (printed F3) | E11 (printed F11) | I2S word select: D2's strip to the amp's LRC. | 40 mm |
| L2 | yellow | E2 (printed F2) | E12 (printed F12) | I2S bit clock: D1's strip to the amp's BCLK. | 50 mm |
| L3 | white | E1 (printed F1) | E13 (printed F13) | Speaker data: D0's strip to the amp's DIN. | 55 mm |
| L4 | red | F1 (printed E1) | E17 (printed F17) | +5 V from the XIAO's 5V strip to the amp's Vin. | 70 mm |
| L5 | black | F2 (printed E2) | E16 (printed F16) | Ground from the XIAO's GND strip to the amp's GND. | 60 mm |
| L6 | red | C14 (printed H14) | C17 (printed H17) | Ties the amp's GAIN to its Vin (+5 V): 6 dB. This is wire J2 in the wire list. | 20 mm |

Cut lengths are the routed length × 1.15 + 10 mm, rounded up to 5 mm. They are estimates: trim each link to fit.

## Where it goes

**Pending.** The mechanical design decides where the carrier mounts, where the mic and the PCA9685 sit on their wires, and the height above the flat amp and the XIAO. The row-1 edge, with the USB-C port, goes to an opening in the robot's outer wall. The XIAO ESP32-C3 has no antenna of its own: its external antenna plugs into the connector by its buttons and needs room. The S3 Sense's camera board stacks on top of the XIAO and needs clearance above it. [`carrier_cad.py`](../tools/carrier/carrier_cad.py) turns this layout into datums for the 3D model.
