Coglet Flasher

Build, flash and test Coglet, a voice-and-servo robot head that agent-hub drives over MCP

Coglet documentation

Switch the servo supply off before you plug in USB. Opening the port can reset the board, and the servo rail switch is the only real emergency stop.

Every boot starts released, and a fresh board has no servo roles assigned, so flashing moves nothing. Calibration lives on the board: "erase" wipes it, so on a robot that's already calibrated, export it first (!coglet export).

1. Build the hardware

Coglet is a Seeed XIAO ESP32, an I²S microphone and amp, and a PCA9685 driving nine hobby servos. Full details, the Fritzing schematic and the bench notes are in WIRING.md.

Parts

  • Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3 (or XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense for the camera build)
  • INMP441 I²S microphone (3.3 V) and MAX98357A I²S amp (5 V) with a 4–8 Ω speaker
  • PCA9685 16-channel servo board at I²C address 0x40
  • Up to nine hobby servos, fitted one at a time from the base up
  • A separate switched 4.8–6 V servo supply. Its switch is the emergency stop

Wiring (the same pads on every board)

ConnectXIAO padC3 GPIOS3 GPIO
Amp DIND021
I²S BCLK: amp BCLK and mic SCKD132
I²S WS: amp LRC and mic WSD243
Mic SD (data out of the mic)D354
PCA9685 SDAD465
PCA9685 SCLD576
PCA9685 /OE (with a pull-up to 3.3 V)D10109

Wire by the pad column. A C3 and an S3 use the same harness; the firmware handles the GPIO numbers. SDA and SCL are on the XIAO's default I²C pads. The bold pairs are the easy ones to swap: with D2/D3 swapped the amp hisses and the mic reads zero; with SDA/SCL swapped nothing answers on the I²C bus.

Power

  • Mic and PCA9685 logic VCC from the XIAO's 3.3 V. Amp Vin from 5 V.
  • Servo V+ goes to the PCA9685's V+ terminal from its own switched supply. Never power servos from the XIAO.
  • All grounds common: XIAO, mic, amp, PCA9685 and the servo supply.

2. Flash the firmware
1
Servo supply off. Plug the XIAO into this computer by USB-C.
2
Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Click the button for your board and pick its port (on Linux usually /dev/ttyACM0).
3
A new board: let it erase. A calibrated robot: export calibration first and install without erasing.
4
If the port doesn't appear, hold the XIAO's BOOT button while plugging it in, then try again.

4. Connect it to agent-hub

You need an agent-hub running and reachable from the robot's Wi-Fi. Its check-in endpoint is on port 8003 by default.

1
Add Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz): !wifi YourSSID YourPassword
2
Point it at the hub: !server http://HUB_HOST:8003/xiaozhi/ota/. Use the hub's real address, not localhost. It reboots.
3
Reconnect the console and run !status. Expect an IP address, a Hub WebSocket URL and Session: listening.
4
Open the hub dashboard. The robot appears under its MAC address (!status shows it as Device id), labelled coglet-c3.
5
Talk to it. The hub greets it once, then say something. The console logs >> what it heard and << what it says, and the reply plays through the speaker. Press the BOOT button to stop a reply.

A hub with an enrollment token: use its HTTPS check-in URL with ?enrollment_token=..., typed here, never baked into a public build.

5. Test its MCP tools

Once the session is up, agent-hub lists the robot's tools. On the dashboard the device shows self.coglet.gaze, blink, animate, stop, release and state, plus self.get_device_status and self.audio_speaker.set_volume.

  • By voice: "What's your volume?" or "Turn your volume down a bit." These use the status and volume tools.
  • Motion tools return errors until a person engages the robot after calibration. That's the safety design: the LLM can stop Coglet but never start it.
  • From Claude Code, through agent-hub's device proxy:
claude mcp add coglet -- python3 /path/to/agent-hub/scripts/device_mcp_proxy.py \
    --device YOUR_ROBOT_MAC

The proxy is on agent-hub's feat/device-mcp-proxy branch until that's merged. It exposes only tools that can't start motion (state, status, stop, release). See AGENT-HUB.md.

6. Calibrate, one servo at a time

Fit one servo on its channel, servo supply on, hand on the switch. Calibration pauses the voice until you say !quiet off.

!coglet fit base          assign base to channel 0 (no movement)
!coglet builder           outputs on, voice paused, still nothing moves
!coglet identify 0        the base should twitch
!coglet explore base      centre it, then:
!coglet nudge -2          ... step toward one end, stop short of binding
!coglet mark low
!coglet nudge 2           ... walk to the other end
!coglet mark high
!coglet confirm base      you watched both ends
!coglet save

Repeat for each servo you fit, then !coglet release and !coglet engage to let agent-hub drive it. Run !quiet off to bring the voice back. Full procedure: CALIBRATION.md.
