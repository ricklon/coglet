Coglet Flasher

Firmware for Coglet, a voice-and-servo robot that agent-hub drives over MCP

Coglet documentation

Switch the servo supply off before you plug in USB. Opening the port can reset the board, and the servo rail switch is the only real emergency stop.

Every boot starts released with no servo roles mapped, so a fresh flash moves nothing. Calibration lives in NVS: an erase-and-install wipes it, so export it first (!coglet export).

1. Flash firmware

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop (Web Serial). Pick the board that is in the robot.

Coglet C3

XIAO ESP32-C3 · 4MB · No camera
INMP441 mic · MAX98357A amp · PCA9685
This browser has no Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge.

Coglet S3

3. Connect it to agent-hub
1
Flash the firmware with the button above, servo supply off.
2
Click Connect and choose the board's port.
3
Add Wi-Fi: !wifi YourSSID YourPassword
4
Point it at your hub: !server http://HUB_HOST:8003/xiaozhi/ota/ (or the hub's HTTPS check-in URL with its enrollment token).
5
The board checks in, opens a voice session, and agent-hub lists its self.coglet.* MCP tools. It shows up on the hub dashboard.
6
Before anything moves, calibrate each servo locally. See CALIBRATION.md.
