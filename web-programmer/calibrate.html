Calibrate the servos

Map and measure each servo before anything is allowed to move.

Calibrate, one servo at a time

Fit one servo on its channel, servo supply on, hand on the switch. Calibration pauses the voice until you say !quiet off.

!coglet fit base          assign base to channel 0 (no movement)
!coglet calibrate         outputs on, voice quiet, still nothing moves
!coglet identify 0        the base should twitch
!coglet explore base      centre it, then:
!coglet nudge -2          ... step toward one end, stop short of binding
!coglet mark low
!coglet nudge 2           ... walk to the other end
!coglet mark high
!coglet confirm base      you watched both ends
!coglet save
!coglet test base         sweeps centre, low, high, centre to check it

Repeat for each servo you fit. !coglet test (in calibration mode) then sweeps every calibrated servo in turn; !coglet release stops it at once. Then !coglet release and !coglet engage to let agent-hub drive it. Run !quiet off to bring the voice back. Full procedure: CALIBRATION.md.
