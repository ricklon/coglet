Coglet

A small talking robot head that agent-hub drives over MCP. Build it, flash it, connect it, and calibrate it, one page at a time.

Switch the servo supply off before you plug in USB. Opening the port can reset the board, and the servo rail switch is the only real emergency stop. Every boot starts released, so flashing moves nothing.

What it is

Coglet has a three-axis neck, two-axis eyes, a shared top-lid servo, a jaw and two ears: nine hobby servos on a PCA9685. A microphone and speaker give it voice, and the S3 board adds a camera. You don't program it: it checks in with agent-hub, opens a voice session, and advertises its abilities as MCP tools. The hub's persona hears what people say and calls tools like self.coglet.gaze on the robot. No servo gives position feedback, so every position reported is the last one commanded.

Work through it in this order
1
Parts and tools. What to gather, and the pad and servo-channel maps.
2
Flash the firmware. From your browser, onto the bare XIAO.
3
Wire it. One wire per step, with a diagram and a check after each stage.
4
Check the board. The serial console and the bench checks. Keep it open in a second tab while you wire.
5
Connect to agent-hub. Wi-Fi, the hub address, and trying the MCP tools. Still no servos.
6
Build the mechanism. The servo fit order. The mechanical steps aren't written yet.
7
Calibrate. Each servo, one at a time, before anything is allowed to move. Alternate with step 6.
Which board

The S3 is the primary board and supports everything. The C3 and C6 can be used with the project but can't do every feature. All three take the same wiring, pad for pad.
