Parts, pads and servo channels

Coglet is a Seeed XIAO ESP32, an I²S microphone and amp, and a PCA9685 driving nine hobby servos. Gather these before you start. The next pages put the firmware on the board, then wire it one wire at a time. Pin and power details are in WIRING.md, which also has the Fritzing drawings.

Parts

  • Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense, the main board (camera and wake word). A XIAO ESP32-C3 or ESP32-C6 also works, without the camera or wake word.
  • INMP441 I²S microphone (3.3 V) and MAX98357A I²S amp (5 V) with a 4–8 Ω speaker
  • PCA9685 16-channel servo board at I²C address 0x40
  • Up to nine hobby servos, fitted one at a time from the base up
  • A separate switched 4.8–6 V servo supply. Its switch is the emergency stop

Tools

  • Soldering iron and solder, for the headers and the wires
  • A multimeter with a continuity setting; the wiring guide has you check each stage
  • Wire strippers, hook-up wire, and a data-capable USB-C cable (some are charge-only)

Wire by the pad column. A C3 and an S3 use the same harness; the firmware handles the GPIO numbers. SDA and SCL are on the XIAO's default I²C pads. The bold pairs are the easy ones to swap: with D2/D3 swapped the amp hisses and the mic reads zero; with SDA/SCL swapped nothing answers on the I²C bus.

Power

  • Mic and PCA9685 logic VCC from the XIAO's 3.3 V. Amp Vin from 5 V.
  • Servo V+ goes to the PCA9685's V+ terminal from its own switched supply. Never power servos from the XIAO.
  • All grounds common: XIAO, mic, amp, PCA9685 and the servo supply.
