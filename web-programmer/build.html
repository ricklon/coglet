Build the mechanism

Fit the servos one at a time, from the base upward.

The mechanical steps aren't written yet. Printed parts, servo mounting, horns, linkages and clearances are still to do, so there is no mechanical guide here. This page gives the order to work in; the electronics and calibration steps are complete.

Fit order

Work through the servos in this order. Each plugs into its standard PCA9685 channel. For every servo: servo switch off, mount it, plug it in, then calibrate it on the Calibrate page before you fit the next.
